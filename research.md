# Short-form video best practice for POS / B2B SaaS (research, Oct 2026)

Note: platform UI changes often; safe-zone figures from third-party guides disagree, so we use the union (most conservative). Official TikTok/Google pages are cited where available; length and hook stats are from marketing blogs (directional, not guaranteed).

## Checklist we follow
- Master file 1080x1920, 9:16, MP4/H.264, 30 fps, 720p minimum, 1080p delivered [1][2].
- Hook in 0-3 s: show the result or a pain line on screen in frame 1; no logo intro; TikTok advises hooking inside the first 6 s, ideally sooner [1][3].
- Length: Reels 15-30 s (cap 90 s for Reels pushed to non-followers), TikTok 20-45 s, Facebook Reels 20-30 s product / 30-45 s how-to, Stories loops 6-15 s [3][4][5].
- Burned-in captions on every video, 5-10 words per second max, large type (>= 56 px), because roughly 7 in 10 viewers watch muted [1][3].
- Safe zone on 1080x1920: keep text, faces, UI focus inside top 250 / bottom 450 / left 80 / right 140 px margins (see Safe zones) [1][6][7].
- Cover: design inside the centre 1080x1440 (3:4 grid crop, 240 px lost top and bottom) and keep headline inside the centre 1010x1440 block [8][9].
- Cut or change visual every 2-4 s (zoom, new screen, text swap); no static shot longer than 4 s [3].
- Last 2-3 s end card: one CTA ("Book a demo" / link in bio), logo, inside safe zone; add a spoken or text CTA earlier too [1].
- Show real product: raw screen recording cropped tight and zoomed on the action, optionally inside a light device frame; lo-fi/native look beats polished ads on TikTok [1].
- Series: fixed title format ("POS in 30 s #n", "X vs Y", "Mistake #n") so viewers follow; one idea per video.
- Google Business Profile: do not rely on posts for video; upload videos (<= 30 s, <= 75 MB, >= 720p) to the Photos/Videos tab, and use still images in posts [10][11].
- Arabic and English variants: text blocks mirrored; numbers stay LTR.

## Safe zones (1080x1920)
- Instagram Reels guides: centre 1080x1440 is safe; bottom ~250 px caption/audio/buttons; top ~100 px; keep right ~10% (about 108 px) free of text and faces [6][7].
- TikTok guides: bottom 150-250 px, top 90-100 px, right 100-120 px (action rail), left 80 px. TikTok for Business ad guide: bottom 150, left 80, top 90 [1][6].
- TikTok description + sound strip often reaches higher (about 20% of height) on organic posts [7].
- Facebook Reels: similar to Reels; bottom caption/CTA area is heavy, keep the same margins [6][7].
- Recommended union: top 250, bottom 450, left 80, right 140. Live text area = x 80-940, y 250-1470.

## Aspect ratios
- 9:16 for Reels, TikTok, FB Reels, Stories. Feed video on Instagram/Facebook: 4:5 (1080x1350) takes the most screen space; 1:1 acceptable [6][8].
- Instagram profile grid is 3:4 (1080x1440) since Jan 2025; Reels cover is cropped from the 9:16 frame [8][9].
- Deliver 9:16 master, and export a 4:5 centre crop only for feed placement (keep text in the centre 1080x1350).

## Length and pacing
- Sweet spots reported: Reels 7-30 s (max longer allowed), TikTok 21-60 s with longer videos gaining reach, FB Reels 20-45 s [3][4][5]. For B2B demos we target 20-35 s.
- Hook every 10 s for videos > 30 s on Facebook [5]. Cut every 2-4 s; vary scale (wide screen, then zoom to button).
- Stories: 6-15 s loops; make last frame lead into the first for a seamless loop (reasonable practice; no official source).

## Hook, text, captions
- Frame 1 carries the claim ("Close the till in 20 seconds"). Text overlay + voice-over or music.
- Burned-in captions plus platform auto-captions off, so they are never hidden by UI; position in the middle third.
- TikTok recommends 5-10 words per second as a ceiling for on-screen text [1].

## End card and CTA
- Structure: hook, key benefit, proof/demo, CTA [1]. Final 2-3 s card with a single action; keep it above the bottom 450 px band.
- Pin a comment or caption with the link/WhatsApp number; use link in bio on IG/TikTok.

## Cover / thumbnail
- 9:16 cover 1080x1920; grid crop keeps y 240-1680 (1080x1440). Centre-safe block for headline/face/logo: 1010x1440 centred (x 35-1045, y 240-1680); stricter "survives everything" band is the middle 55-60% of height (about y 770-1150 core) [8][9].
- Cover text: 3-5 words, high contrast, same template across a series.

## Mockup vs raw screen recording
- TikTok favours native, unpolished content [1]. Use raw recordings zoomed on the action, with a simple device frame only for wide shots; avoid tiny full-screen captures where text is unreadable on a phone.

## Comparison and series formats
- "X vs Y" split screen (paper ledger vs Leap POS, old till vs offline-first): left/right or top/bottom halves, label each half, reveal the result in the final 3 s. Keep labels out of the right 140 px and bottom 450 px bands.
- Recurring series: "30-second feature", "Myth vs fact", "Day in the shop", "Setup in 5 minutes". Consistent cover template and title.

## Google Business Profile
- Posts (Updates, Offers, Events): Google's posts help says an update can include description, photo, video and a button [11]; in practice some API tools and older guides report video is not shown in posts [12]. Treat posts as photo-first and verify in our own profile before relying on video.
- Photos: JPG/PNG, 10 KB-5 MB, recommended 720x720 px, minimum 250x250 px [10].
- Video: up to 30 s, up to 75 MB, 720p or higher; can take 24-48 h to appear; profile must be verified [10].
- Use instead / also: upload the video through Photos > Videos, use a 1:1 or 4:3 still (with text) for posts, and put the Reel link in the post's action button where video does not render.

## Launching new or empty pages (added Oct 2026)

Checklist we follow
- Seed before promoting: a visitor who lands on an empty profile leaves. Put at least 6–9 posts on each profile (one full 3-wide Instagram row set) before inviting followers, running ads or printing the handle anywhere [13][14].
- Instagram 9-grid first: the top 9 tiles act as the "home page". Plan them as 3 rows of 3 (row 1 = who we are / hero video / WhatsApp CTA; row 2 = desktop vs web / offline / multi-country; row 3 = Arabic / inventory / reports) and publish in reverse order so they land in the right place [13].
- Cadence for a new account: daily for the first month is fine as long as quality holds. Buffer's data: Instagram 3–5 posts/week roughly doubles follower growth vs 1–2, and 6–9/week grows faster still with diminishing returns; TikTok 2–5/week gives the biggest lift, more posts mainly add "lottery tickets" [15][16]. We run 1 feed post/day on FB, IG and TikTok, plus 2 Stories/week.
- Google Business Profile: 1–3 updates per week is the recommended range; weekly activity is what matters, more does not help [17][18]. We cap it at 3/week.
- Times (Asia/Beirut): Lebanon and the Gulf scroll late. UAE data: Instagram 16:00–18:00 GST, Facebook 14:00–16:00 GST, TikTok 21:00–23:00 GST [19]. The Gulf weekend differs (UAE Sat–Sun, KSA/Qatar Fri–Sat), so Friday evening is the one slot that is "weekend" everywhere [20]. We use FB 12:30 or 19:30, IG 13:00 or 20:30, TikTok 21:00 (14:00 for a 2nd daily post in the launch window), GBP 12:00 on weekdays.
- Spacing: never two posts on the same platform less than 4 hours apart (Leap Health posts share the same Leap Coder FB/IG accounts at 10:00–12:00/19:30, so count those too); keep cross-platform versions 30–60 min apart so the team can answer comments.
- Mix for a new profile: ~40% product proof (real screens), ~30% problem/tips/FAQ, ~20% trust (how it works, behind the scenes), ~10% direct CTA. About a third in Arabic.
